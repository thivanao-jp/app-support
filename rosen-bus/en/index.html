How to play

Open How to play in the menu for choosing routes, creating and deleting custom routes, and controls during a ride.

Purchases and restoration

Before buying, open Expansion Pack in the app to check the included features and price. To restore purchased features, select Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Display language and announcements

Choose your display language under Display language in Settings. Turn on English after Japanese to hear English after each Japanese announcement finishes. This setting is independent of the display language and also works with the Japanese interface.

Internet access and graphics

Maps, terrain, aerial photos and buildings require an internet connection. If rendering is slow, choose Low under Graphics in Settings. Changing graphics quality reloads the route and returns to the departure screen.

Data sources

  • Terrain, aerial photos and maps: Geospatial Information Authority of Japan
  • Buildings: MLIT Project PLATEAU
  • Roads and other geographic data: © OpenStreetMap contributors

Sources and licenses for each route are listed under Data Sources in the app. Routes, stops and structures include estimates and simplifications, and timetables are adjusted for the experience. Do not use the app for real travel information. This is an unofficial app, not affiliated with any transport operator.

Geographic and route data

The processed database containing OpenStreetMap-derived data is available free under ODbL 1.0. Program code, scenery images and users' saved routes and settings are excluded.
