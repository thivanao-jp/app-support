Privacy Policy

This page explains how Mousou Bus Ride handles information.

Information stored on your device

The app stores custom route names, stop names, points, directions and other route settings; display language; the English-announcement setting; sound, graphics and orientation preferences; and selected bus paint/patterns on your device. The app has no account registration or developer-operated storage server.

These services receive connection information such as the requested tile/file URL, IP address and time of access. URLs may reveal the virtual area being viewed in the app. GSI states that it collects IP addresses and browsing information in access logs. Each provider's policies govern its connection data and logs. The app developer does not obtain these providers' access logs.

Tiles are chosen from the route and virtual view position. The app does not request your device's current location. It does not send custom route names, stop names or saved preferences to scenery providers.

Purchases and restoration

The Expansion Pack is a non-consumable in-app purchase using Apple's App Store and StoreKit. Product lookup, purchase and restoration communicate with Apple. Apple handles Apple Account and payment information under its own policies. The developer does not receive payment details such as card numbers.

The app determines access on your device from transactions verified by StoreKit. It has no separate purchase account and no function that sends transaction information to a developer-operated server. See App Store & Privacy for Apple's handling of store information.

Announcements

Announcements use your device's speech synthesis. They may read built-in stop names and names you enter for custom stops. English after Japanese adds English speech after the Japanese announcement. The app does not record or accept microphone input. See Apple's privacy information for OS and voice-related practices.

Advertising and analytics

There are no ads or subscriptions. The app includes no advertising or access-analytics SDK. External connections for scenery and purchases/restoration work as described above.

Guide and deleting saved routes

The guide text and illustrations are bundled in the app. To delete a saved route, open its area in Build by area, then select Delete beside it under Routes saved in this area.
