Privacy policy

This policy describes how Daydream Rail 3D handles information.

Information the app collects

Daydream Rail 3D does not require an account and does not collect personal data. It does not include advertising or usage analytics.

Map and scenery requests

The app requests public map and terrain data from the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan and city model data from Project PLATEAU. It may request public route data through OpenStreetMap’s Overpass API. These requests retrieve data for the area being displayed.

Routes, settings and files

Routes and settings are stored on your device. When you choose to share a route or a video, iOS sends the exported file to the destination you select. Route files contain the route geometry, station names, descriptions and portable settings. Review any text you entered before sharing it.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases and purchase restoration through the App Store. The app uses the resulting entitlement to unlock the pack.

Questions or updates

For questions about this policy, email support. We may update this policy when the app changes.
