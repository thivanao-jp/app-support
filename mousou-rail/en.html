Daydream Rail 3D

Build and drive your own railway through real Japanese terrain and city scenery. Place stations, adjust the track, and record a trip as a video.

Using the app

  • Choose a region and browse routes by map, search or route feature. Tap a route to edit, drive or record it.
  • Choose Free scenery trial for the fixed Yurikamome Rainbow Bridge experience. Standard route editing and driving are also free.
  • Open a route’s details and tap Share to export a route file. Use Import on the home screen to add a route from a file.
  • To recover a prior purchase, open Scenery & Vehicle Pack and tap Restore purchases using the Apple Account originally used to buy it.
Privacy policy Email support

For help, include the app version, device model, and steps to reproduce the issue. Please omit personal information from route files or screenshots.

Map and scenery data

  • Map, terrain and imagery from the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan, with an overview map using Natural Earth.
  • 3D city data from the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism’s Project PLATEAU.
  • Railway and scenery data © OpenStreetMap contributors. See Data credits in the app for source and license details.

Routes may be fictional or adapted. Scenery and structures are simplified, and available detail varies by location.
