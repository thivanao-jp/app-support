プライバシーポリシー / Privacy Policy

Daydream Ski Support

Hold the left or right half to turn, or both to snowplow. Swipe up to hop. Double-tap to lock or unlock tuck. Enable tilt controls in the menu. Portrait and landscape are supported.

Lift rides are free. The first play after launch can skip immediately; subsequent rides unlock a free skip after three towers. Optional rewarded ads offer an earlier skip. Banner ads appear during lift rides. Pro removes ads and allows immediate skips.

For missing purchases, use the same Apple Account and choose Pro & outfits → Restore Purchases. Pro and the outfit pack are separate products.

Use the recovery prompt or return to the start through the menu if you leave the course. For support, include your device, OS/app version, region and steps to reproduce. Do not send passwords or payment details. Contact: thivanao@gmail.com.
